### Create a formula in cell D3 that calculates the cost of flakes eaten using the information below to help you:

> Flake Total  =  Quantity  *  Price Each

> Copy the formula down the rest of the column to calculate the cost of all the other chocolates.

### Create a formula in cell E3 that calculates the Vat Total for the flakes using the information below to help you:

> Flake Vat Total  =  Total  *  17.5%

> Copy the formula down the rest of the column to calculate the Vat Total of all the other chocolates.

### Create a formula in cell F3 that calculates the Final cost for the flakes using the information below to help you:

> Flake Final  =  Total  +  Vat Total

> Copy the formula down the rest of the column to calculate the Final cost of all the other chocolates.
